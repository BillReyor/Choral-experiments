About Palare Piano

Real voices, where added, come from VocalSet (Wilkins, Seetharaman, Wahl and Pardo; CC BY 4.0), re-voiced with Seed-VC. Tracks made with them come with a Credits file: keep it with them if you share them.

Palare Piano is free software under the GNU General Public License, version 3. Palare is one spelling of Polari, the slang gay men in Britain once spoke so as not to be overheard; piano means softly.

It keeps running quietly after you close this window, so that long jobs can finish.

A practice track is for learning your part. The singing stays with the singers.
